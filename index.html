Keys: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Keys is a piano practice app. This policy says what the app does with your data. The short version: we do not run accounts, ads or tracking, and we do not keep your recordings.

What the app uses

Microphone. Keys listens through the microphone for two things only:

  • Practice mode. While you play, the app analyses the sound on your device to check whether you played the expected notes. This audio is processed on your device and is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Record. When you tap Record, the app records the room audio until you stop. It sends that recording over the internet to our transcription server, which turns it into notes (MIDI and sheet music) and sends the notes back. The server deletes the recording as soon as the notes are produced. We do not keep, listen to, share or sell recordings.

Files you import. MIDI and MusicXML files you choose are read on your device only.

MIDI piano. If you connect a USB or Bluetooth MIDI piano, the app reads the notes you play. This stays on your device.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking of what you do in the app.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Data sent to the server

Only the recording you start with Record, and only when you tap it. The transcription server may log technical details such as the time of a request and its size. It does not keep the audio or the notes.

Songs and copyright

Record is meant for your own playing and music in your room. You are responsible for having the right to record what you record. Keys does not include copyrighted songs.

Children

Keys is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can deny microphone access in your device settings. Practice with microphone and Record then will not work, but you can still listen to songs and use a MIDI piano.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
